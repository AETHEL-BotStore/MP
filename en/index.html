02 / MAIN PROJECTAETHEL
MY PRODUCT SINCE 2025

Your business, your own bot.
One system behind it.

AETHEL automatically connects a separate Telegram bot for every professional or studio. Clients choose a service and an available time themselves, while the business manages bookings, schedules, and customer data in its CRM.

One platform powers many individual bots. Each business keeps its own identity and can launch without the cost of custom development from scratch.
